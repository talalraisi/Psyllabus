## The seven learning outcomes

- `3.1` Identify your own strengths and areas for growth
- `3.1` Undertake new challenges and develop new skills
- `3.1` Initiate and plan a CAS experience
- `3.1` Show perseverance and commitment
- `3.2` Demonstrate the skills and benefits of working collaboratively
- `3.2` Engage with issues of global significance
- `3.2` Recognise and consider the ethics of choices and actions

## The three strands

- `1.1` Arts and creative thinking experiences
- `1.1` Designing, making or performing something new
- `1.1` Developing a creative skill over time
- `1.2` Physical exertion and a healthy lifestyle
- `1.2` Individual fitness and skill development
- `1.2` Team sport and collaborative activity
- `1.3` Identifying a genuine community need
- `1.3` Direct, indirect, advocacy and research service
- `1.3` Reciprocity, respect and dignity in service
- `1.3` Avoiding service that helps the helper more than the community
- `1.4` Balancing the three strands across 18 months
- `1.4` Regular commitment rather than one-off events
- `1.4` Managing CAS alongside academic work
