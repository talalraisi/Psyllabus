# How to check these

Every subject we claim to cover, written out exactly as it sits in the
database. Nobody has ever read these against a real guide — the map was
imported once and has been trusted ever since, and every question we generate
inherits whatever is wrong in it.

## What to look for

Open a subject next to its official guide and mark four things:

**Missing.** A topic or subtopic the guide has and we do not. This is the worst
kind of error because the app will tell a student they have covered everything
while a whole area sits unmapped.

**Renamed.** We have the content under an old name. The student searches for
what their teacher called it and finds nothing.

**Retired.** We have something the current guide no longer examines. Questions
generated here waste a student's evening on material that cannot come up.

**Wrongly levelled.** Something marked HL only that SL students actually sit,
or the reverse.

## Where the risk is concentrated

The IB rewrote several subjects for first exams 2025, and our map does reflect
that — Physics is on themes A to E, Chemistry on Structure and Reactivity,
Biology on A to D, Computer Science on A to H. That is the current shape, not
the old numbered topics, so the big structural risk is already handled.

What has never been checked is the detail inside each theme, and the subjects
nobody has looked at twice.

Thin subjects are worth a glance first, because a small number usually means an
incomplete import rather than a small course:

| subject | subtopics |
| --- | ---: |
| Dance | 8 |
| Art and Design | 9 |
| Film Studies | 10 |
| Drama and Theatre Studies | 11 |
| Music Technology | 12 |
| Media Studies | 12 |
| Physical Education | 12 |
| Music | 13 |

Some of those are genuinely small, portfolio-assessed courses. Some are
probably half-imported. Only somebody with the guide can tell which.

## When you find something wrong

Note it in the markdown file directly. Do not edit the database by hand — the
files here are generated by `scripts/export-syllabus.mjs` and would be
overwritten. Corrections get applied as a migration once we know the full list.

## The other folder

`context/` holds the same tree in the shape `docs/syllabus-context-spec.md`
describes, with every context field null. That is the work that makes the
question generator accurate. Checking the map comes first, because filling in
context for a subtopic that should not exist is wasted effort.
