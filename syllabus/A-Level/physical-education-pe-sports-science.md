# Physical Education (PE) / Sports Science

IB · 3 topics · 12 subtopics

This is what Project Syllabus has mapped for this subject, exactly as
it sits in the database. Hold it next to the official guide and mark

## Topic 1 - Factors Affecting Participation in Physical Activity and Sport

- Applied anatomy and physiology including the cardiovascular and respiratory systems
- Skill acquisition analyzing theories of learning information processing and guidance
- Sport and society exploring the historical development of modern sport and sporting ethics
- The impact of commercialization media and technology on sport and society

## Topic 2 - Factors Affecting Optimal Performance in Physical Activity and Sport

- Biomechanical movement analyzing force motion fluid mechanics and projectile motion
- Exercise physiology investigating diet nutrition energy systems and injury prevention
- Group dynamics goal setting leadership and building self-efficacy in sports teams
- Sports psychology examining personality attitudes arousal anxiety and motivation

## Topic 3 - Practical Performance in Physical Activity and Sport

- Application of theoretical physiological and psychological causes to real performance issues
- Demonstration of technical proficiency tactical awareness and physical conditioning
- Practical performance as either a player performer or coach in one fully competitive activity
- Written evaluation and analysis of performance for improvement identifying physical weaknesses
