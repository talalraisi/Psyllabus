## Topic 1 - Varieties of Film and Filmmaking

- American film since 2005 comparing mainstream and contemporary independent film
- British film since 1995 analyzing narrative structure and cultural representation
- Hollywood 1930 to 1990 comparative study of classical and new Hollywood cinema

## Topic 2 - Global Filmmaking Perspectives

- Documentary film analyzing the significance of realism objective truth and digital technologies
- Film movements Experimental film 1960 to 2000 analyzing avant-garde aesthetics
- Film movements Silent cinema exploring expressionism aesthetics and early montage
- Global film exploring two distinct international cinema movements and non-English texts

## Topic 3 - Production

- Application of core cinematic concepts cinematography editing sound and mise-en-scene
- Evaluative analysis comparing the student production against professionally produced short films
- Practical production of either a short film or a screenplay accompanied by a storyboard
