# Music Technology

IB · 4 topics · 12 subtopics

This is what Project Syllabus has mapped for this subject, exactly as
it sits in the database. Hold it next to the official guide and mark
anything missing, renamed or no longer examined.

## Topic 1 - Recording

- Application of microphones placement polar patterns DI boxes and audio interfaces
- Production tools and techniques to capture record and mix a multi-track recording
- Use of mixing techniques including EQ compression panning and spatial effects

## Topic 2 - Technology-based Composition

- Application of synthesis sampling and MIDI sequencing techniques and programming
- Creating editing and structuring sounds to develop a technology-based composition
- Creative manipulation of audio and implementation of dynamic and spatial processing

## Topic 3 - Listening and Analysing

- Application of knowledge to analyze unfamiliar commercial tracks and recording scenarios
- Knowledge and understanding of recording and production techniques and audio principles
- The historical development of recording and production technology since the 1950s

## Topic 4 - Producing and Analysing

- Advanced audio manipulation using pitch correction time-stretching and noise reduction
- Editing mixing and production techniques applied to provided raw audio material
- Evaluative understanding of audio acoustics signal flow and final mastering principles
