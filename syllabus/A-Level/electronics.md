## Topic 1 - System Synthesis and Logic Systems

- Boolean algebra De Morgans laws and simplification using Karnaugh maps
- Designing synchronous and asynchronous counters and decoding logic
- Sequential logic applications of flip-flops shift registers and timing diagrams
- System synthesis sub-systems logic gates and Boolean logic operations

## Topic 2 - DC Circuits and Operational Amplifiers

- DC electrical circuits Ohms law Kirchhoffs laws and power calculations
- Input and output sub-systems sensing environmental changes using LDRs and thermistors
- Operational amplifiers inverting non-inverting difference and summing configurations
- Timing circuits calculating time constants in RC networks and astable 555 timers

## Topic 3 - Semiconductors and AC Circuits

- AC circuits alternating currents capacitors inductors and reactance
- High power switching using bipolar junction transistors (BJTs) and MOSFETs
- Passive filters designing low-pass and high-pass filters and interpreting Bode plots
- Semiconductor components characteristics of diodes zener diodes and bridge rectifiers

## Topic 4 - Microcontrollers and Communications

- Analogue and digital communication systems modulation transmission and multiplexing
- Microcontroller architecture input/output memory and clock speeds
- Optical communication properties of optical fibers attenuation and signal degradation
- Programming microcontrollers using flowcharts assembly or C programming languages

## Topic 5 - Mains Power Supply Systems

- Mains power supply circuits transformers rectification and smoothing capacitors
- Voltage regulation zener diode regulation and integrated circuit regulators
