## Topic 1 - Drama and Theatre

- Analysis and critical evaluation of the work of live theatre makers and live productions
- Knowledge and understanding of historical drama contexts and theatrical practices
- Study of one set play from List A exploring theatrical interpretation and acting
- Study of one set play from List B exploring directorial overview and stage design

## Topic 2 - Creating Original Drama

- Application of theatrical skills to realize the devised piece in a live performance
- Creation of a working notebook detailing the devising process research and evaluation
- Devising an original piece of drama influenced by the work of one prescribed practitioner

## Topic 3 - Making Theatre

- Methodology of a prescribed theatrical practitioner applied specifically to Extract 3
- Performance realization of Extract 3 as a performer lighting sound set or costume designer
- Practical exploration and interpretation of three extracts each taken from a different play
- Reflective report evaluating the theatrical interpretation and exploration of all three extracts
