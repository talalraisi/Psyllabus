# Accounting

## Topic 1 - Financial Accounting Core Principles

- Application of fundamental accounting concepts accruals prudence and going concern
- The double-entry model accounting equations and verification of accounting records
- The role of the accountant professional ethics and types of business organization

## Topic 2 - Preparation of Financial Statements

- Accounting for organizations with incomplete records and calculating missing figures
- Preparation of income statements and statements of financial position for sole traders
- Preparation of limited company accounts including share capital and reserves
- Preparation of partnership accounts including appropriation and current accounts

## Topic 3 - Interpretation and Communication of Accounting Information

- Analysis and evaluation of financial information using profitability and liquidity ratios
- Efficiency gearing and investment ratios applied to limited companies
- The limitations of ratio analysis and the impact of ethical considerations on reporting

## Topic 4 - Management Accounting and Decision Making

- Absorption and activity-based costing methods for overhead apportionment
- Budgeting processes cash budgets and the behavioral aspects of budgetary control
- Capital investment appraisal including payback net present value and average rate of return
- Marginal costing contribution break-even analysis and short-term decision making
- Standard costing calculating and interpreting material labor and sales variances
