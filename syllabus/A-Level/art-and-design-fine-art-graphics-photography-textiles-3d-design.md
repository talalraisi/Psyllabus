## Topic 1 - Personal Investigation

- Development of ideas through sustained and focused investigations in response to a starting point
- Exploration of appropriate resources media materials techniques and processes
- Personal presentation of a meaningful artistic response and finalized practical outcome
- Production of a continuous written related study of between 1000 and 3000 words
- Recording of ideas observations and insights relevant to practical intentions

## Topic 2 - Externally Set Assignment

- Investigation of contextual and visual sources related to the chosen starting point
- Preparatory period exploring the examination board provided stimulus paper
- Supervised examination time of 15 hours to produce a finalized practical outcome
- Sustained development and refinement of practical ideas and media application
