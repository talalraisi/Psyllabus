## Topic 1 - Core Technical Principles

- Digital design and manufacture application of CAD CAM and rapid prototyping
- Enhancement of materials forming redistribution and addition manufacturing processes
- Health and safety requirements protecting intellectual property and enterprise
- Identifying and applying materials woods metals polymers composites and smart materials
- Performance characteristics mechanical and physical properties of specific materials
- The use of surface finishes and modern industrial and commercial scale practice

## Topic 2 - Core Designing and Making Principles

- Critical analysis and evaluation of commercial products and manufacturing systems
- Design methods and processes iterative design user-centered design and systems thinking
- Design theory analyzing the work of influential designers and historical design movements
- Socio-economic environmental and cultural impacts on the work of designers

## Topic 3 - Non-Exam Assessment (NEA) Practical Application

- Development of design proposals through iterative modeling testing and refinement
- Evaluating the final prototype against the specification and analyzing user feedback
- Identifying and investigating complex design possibilities and genuine user needs
- Producing a comprehensive design brief and objective product specification
- Realization and manufacture of a high-quality final working prototype
