## Topic 1 - Meeting Nutritional Needs of Specific Groups

- Planning nutritional requirements for target groups across different life stages and medical conditions
- Preparing and cooking complex dishes demonstrating advanced culinary skills to meet specific dietary needs
- Understanding the properties of macronutrients micronutrients and their physiological functions
- Understanding the relationship between nutrients energy balance and the human gastrointestinal tract

## Topic 2 - Ensuring Food is Safe to Eat

- Food safety legislation the role of the Environmental Health Officer and HACCP systems
- Practical implementation of food safety controls during high-risk food preparation and storage
- Understanding how environmental conditions temperature pH and moisture influence bacterial growth
- Understanding how micro-organisms pathogenic bacteria yeasts and molds affect food safety

## Topic 3 - Experimenting to Solve Food Production Problems

- Designing and conducting scientific investigations to solve specific food production problems
- Evaluating the outcomes of experimental methodology using sensory analysis and objective testing
- The scientific reactions during food preparation gelatinization denaturation and the Maillard reaction
- Understanding the chemical and functional properties of carbohydrates proteins and fats

## Topic 4 - Current Issues in Food Science and Nutrition

- Analyzing the impact of technological developments sustainable food production and food security
- Interpreting primary and secondary data to draw informed conclusions on a contemporary food issue
- Investigating current dietary trends diet-related diseases and public health initiatives
