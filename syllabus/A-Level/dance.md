## Topic 1 - Performance and Choreography

- Application of physical technical and expressive skills in live stage performance
- Group choreography adapting an externally set stimulus into an original dance work
- Performance in a quartet demonstrating spatial awareness and complex ensemble skills
- Solo performance linked to a specified practitioner within a chosen area of study

## Topic 2 - Critical Engagement

- Critical analysis of the movement components aural setting and physical setting
- Historical and cultural context of the compulsory set work and its core choreographer
- Knowledge and understanding of the compulsory historical set work Rooster or Sutra
- Study of an optional area of study focusing on a specific dance era movement or company
