## Topic 1 - Appraising Music

- Listening analysis and contextual understanding of the Western classical tradition 1650 to 1910
- Study of Art music since 1910 including avant-garde and experimental techniques
- Study of Contemporary traditional music and world music fusion aesthetics
- Study of Jazz spanning traditional swing bebop and contemporary styles
- Study of Music for media exploring film television and video game scores
- Study of Music for theatre examining musical theatre works and historical composers
- Study of Pop music including historical development genres and key contemporary artists

## Topic 2 - Performance

- Performance demonstrating control of technique phrasing and sustained musical expression
- Preparation of a solo or ensemble recital showcasing instrumental or vocal proficiency
- Understanding and communication of stylistic conventions appropriate to the chosen repertoire

## Topic 3 - Composition

- Application of core musical elements harmony melody rhythm and instrumentation
- Composition one in response to an externally set brief assessing technique and structural control
- Composition two as a free composition demonstrating personal musical development and identity
