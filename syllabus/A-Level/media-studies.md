# Media Studies

IB · 3 topics · 12 subtopics

This is what Project Syllabus has mapped for this subject, exactly as
it sits in the database. Hold it next to the official guide and mark
anything missing, renamed or no longer examined.

## Topic 1 - Media One

- Close study of advertising marketing music videos and newspapers
- Media audiences how audiences are categorized targeted and respond to texts
- Media industries the influence of ownership funding and regulation on products
- Media language how media texts use semiotics and technical codes to create meaning
- Media representations how individuals groups and social issues are portrayed

## Topic 2 - Media Two

- Application of academic media theories including structuralism postmodernism and feminism
- In-depth study of magazines examining historical contexts and contemporary publishing
- In-depth study of online social and participatory media and the video games industry
- In-depth study of television analyzing global reach broadcasting and long-form narrative

## Topic 3 - Cross-Media Production

- Application of knowledge to create a cross-media production responding to a set brief
- Practical skills in audio-visual print or online digital production and platform integration
- Statement of intent outlining the application of theoretical frameworks to the practical work
