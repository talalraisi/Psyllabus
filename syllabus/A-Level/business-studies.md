# Business Studies

IB · 9 topics · 19 subtopics

This is what Project Syllabus has mapped for this subject, exactly as
it sits in the database. Hold it next to the official guide and mark
anything missing, renamed or no longer examined.

## Topic 1 - What is Business

- Understanding that businesses operate within a changing external environment
- Understanding the nature and purpose of business and different business forms

## Topic 2 - Managers Leadership and Decision Making

- Understanding management leadership styles and decision-making processes
- Understanding the role and importance of internal and external stakeholders

## Topic 3 - Decision Making to Improve Marketing Performance

- Making marketing decisions using segmentation targeting and positioning
- Making marketing decisions using the extended marketing mix (7Ps)
- Setting marketing objectives and understanding markets and customers

## Topic 4 - Decision Making to Improve Operational Performance

- Making operational decisions to improve quality efficiency and manage supply chains
- Setting operational objectives and analyzing operational performance data

## Topic 5 - Decision Making to Improve Financial Performance

- Making financial decisions identifying sources of finance and improving cash flow
- Setting financial objectives and analyzing financial performance using ratio analysis

## Topic 6 - Decision Making to Improve Human Resource Performance

- Making decisions on organizational design motivation and employee engagement
- Setting human resource objectives and analyzing human resource performance

## Topic 7 - Analysing the Strategic Position of a Business

- Analyzing corporate objectives mission statements and internal financial positioning
- Analyzing the external environment using PESTLE and competitive environment analysis

## Topic 8 - Choosing Strategic Direction and Strategic Methods

- Assessing strategic methods including changes in scale innovation and internationalization
- Choosing which markets to compete in and what products to offer using Ansoffs matrix

## Topic 9 - Managing Strategic Change

- Managing organizational culture and the causes of resistance to change
- Managing strategic implementation and understanding why business strategies fail
